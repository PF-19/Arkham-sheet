Roll log

With 2 dice, the sum and the highest die are both shown, each plus the chosen skill. Use whichever the book's instruction calls for.

Experience dice (between adventures)

Per the publisher: at the start of your next adventure, roll one die for each star you earned. Each 6 lets you permanently raise one stat. Each 1 forces you to permanently lower one stat. Pick any stats that changed temporarily in the last adventure first.
